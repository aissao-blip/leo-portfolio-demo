# Demo walkthrough — 60 to 90 second video script

Record at 1440×900. Run `python run.py`, open `http://127.0.0.1:5055`, and
press **Reset demo data** first so the numbers match this script.

Total: 85 seconds.

---

### 0:00 – 0:12 · The dashboard

> "This is Leo, an AI agent for creator partnership operations. Twenty-two
> creators, six active partnerships, and five emails that need a decision
> today."

*On screen: the dashboard. Let the six stat cards land, then move the cursor
down the "Needs a decision" column.*

> "The important thing isn't the count. It's that every row says why it's
> there."

---

### 0:12 – 0:32 · The inbox, and the reasoning

*Click **Inbox**.*

> "Most inboxes sort by sender or folder. Leo sorts by what each message is
> asking of you — because at nine in the morning that's the only question
> that matters."

*Hover the reason line under Rosa Castellanos.*

> "It reads as negotiating terms, because it matched 'room to move' and
> 'closer to'. You can disagree with that in one read, which you can't do
> with a label on its own."

---

### 0:32 – 0:50 · Drafting, and the limit

*Click **Draft a reply** on the negotiation email. The draft appears.*

> "It drafts the reply using what it knows about that creator — their tier,
> their fee, what they asked. And then it stops."

*Point at "not sent".*

> "It has not sent anything. It can't. There's no mail transport in this
> application at all. The agent prepares; the person decides."

*Click **Approve**.*

> "Approving marks the thread handled and moves the creator to Negotiating."

---

### 0:50 – 1:08 · The outreach guard

*Click **Outreach**. Scroll to "Stopped because they replied".*

> "Three-stage sequence, five days apart. And the part I care most about —
> it stops the moment someone replies."

*Click **Try to advance** on one of those rows. The refusal toast appears.*

> "That's a rule in the handler, not a reminder. Chasing someone who already
> answered you is the most embarrassing thing this kind of software does, so
> it's made impossible rather than discouraged."

---

### 1:08 – 1:20 · Campaigns and the briefing

*Click **Campaigns**, then **Briefing**.*

> "Campaign deliverables per creator with their dates. And a daily briefing
> that's computed from the current state, not stored — change anything and it
> changes with it."

---

### 1:20 – 1:25 · Close on the activity log

*Click **Activity**.*

> "And every action is labelled: simulated, meaning the agent decided
> something, or real, meaning something actually changed. A demo that blurs
> those two isn't worth trusting."

## If you only have 30 seconds

Dashboard → Inbox → draft → approve → the outreach refusal. That sequence
carries the whole argument: it reads, it reasons out loud, it prepares, it
refuses to overstep.
